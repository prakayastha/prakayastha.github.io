## Journal

This is a space to collect my weekly logs on things I've been thinking about, things that excite me, or things that I want to learn. Like many things in my life, this exercise is also inspired by [Lucy](https://lucydot.github.io/news/).   
Being an academic can sometimes feel like nothing fruitful has happened if something isn't published every other month, but that obviously isn't true. Good science takes time, so here's a record of some non-linear thinking. 

7th Oct 2026:    
Caught the freshers flu (LOL). But I am fully locked in to get something solid to present in Vienna in the next couple of weeks. It is also Nobel Prize week! Every year I am humbled on how little I know about physics and/or chemistry. 

On the 10th, it will be a year since I defended my PhD thesis, and I have been reflecting a lot. I am thinking of making a "Things I learnt from my PhD journey" video over the weekend... 

1st Oct 2026:    
Start of the new term at UCL, and I've already met some enthusiastic master's students in the Chemistry department. The good news is that postdocs can now teach/demonstrate, which is great for some of us who are seriously thinking about an academic career ahead... I am also part of the first-ever (!!) postdoc committee that the department has ever had. Mostly I joined this because, believe it or not, postdoc life is even more isolating than PhD life, so I thought this was a nice way of community building. 

Keith and I are also heavily discussing my post-processing results on the quin systems and planning out the next few weeks of work.

22nd Sept 2026:    
After a few weeks of Young downtime, I was finally able to run some long MD on the quin structures. I'm getting quite close to interpreting the order-disorder transitions through angular projections... could this be the first exciting(!) result of my postdoc? 

16th Sept 2026:      
I've worked on computing Raman spectra for a good few years now but never for hybrid organic-inorganic systems. This week I'm trying to reproduce the experimental results of some hybrid organic-inorganic perovskite-like structures. 

8th Sept 2026:     
Brad and I are delivering an "Introduction to Git and Github/Gitlab" session in Liverpool next week, so I'm working on making the slides/exercises for that over the next couple of days (repo hosted [here](https://github.com/mdi-group/intro-to-git-workshop/tree/main)). 

3rd Sept 2026:   
This week I've been working on generating reasonable MLIP-MD and AIMD trajectories for the quin structures. It's been quite a challenge thinking about fast H-dynamics, molecular solids, and entropy-driven phase transitions.
